# Compliance Rules

Full guidance: `docs/copy-and-compliance.md`.

## Health claims

- Never write that a product cures, treats, heals, prevents, reverses or fights a disease or condition. Never name diseases next to a product.
- Product descriptions, ingredients and usage render **only** the Sheet's approved fields. Components must not add benefit copy.
- Every product page and article shows the health disclaimer.
- Testimonials describe service and experience, not medical outcomes.

## Brand

- The site logo is the distributor's own wordmark. "Independent KEDI Distributor" appears with it in the header and in the footer.
- KEDI's logo may appear only inside the "Genuine KEDI products" badge, never as the site logo.
- About page states: "We are an independent distributor of KEDI Healthcare products."

## Business opportunity

- No income figures, guarantees, "financial freedom", "get rich", lifestyle imagery (cars, cash) or recruitment pressure.

## Data protection (NDPA 2023)

- Every form has an unticked consent checkbox that must be ticked to submit.
- Collect only what the form needs. No date of birth, no health conditions.
- Analytics and pixels load only after the visitor accepts cookies.
- Privacy policy linked next to every consent checkbox.
