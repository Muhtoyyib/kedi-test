# Copy and Compliance

## 1. Voice

- **Warm, plain, local.** Write like a knowledgeable neighbour, not a pharmacy leaflet or a hype ad. "We'll help you choose", not "Unlock optimal wellness".
- **Short sentences.** Aim for a reading age of about 12. One idea per sentence.
- **"We" and "you".** The business is a real person and team: "[Distributor Name] will reply within the hour."
- **Nigerian context.** Naira, local areas, WhatsApp-first, bank transfer. Avoid Americanisms like "zip code" and "checkout".
- **Calm confidence.** No exclamation marks in headings. At most one per page in body copy.
- **Sentence case** for headings and buttons.

## 2. Health-claim rules (mandatory)

Health and wellness advertising in Nigeria is regulated, and Meta's ad policies restrict health claims. The site must pass both.

**Never:**

- say a product **cures, treats, heals, prevents, reverses, fights, eliminates or protects against** any disease or condition;
- name a disease or medical condition next to a product (e.g. diabetes, hypertension, ulcer, infertility, cancer, infection, arthritis);
- promise outcomes or timelines ("results in 7 days", "lose 5 kg");
- use before/after photos, or testimonials describing medical results;
- show clinical imagery (pills spilling, syringes, hospital beds, doctors with stethoscopes);
- use "doctor recommended", "clinically proven" or "100% natural so no side effects".

**Product text:** only the Sheet's approved fields (`short_benefit`, `description`, `ingredients`, `how_to_use`, product FAQs). Components never add benefit language around them.

**Articles:** educate about habits (food, water, sleep, movement, stress) and when to see a doctor. They may link to a product category ("Explore our digestive health range") but not claim a product fixes anything.

**Allowed CTA patterns:** "Ask us which product fits your routine", "Learn how to use it correctly", "Join this week's free seminar".

### Health disclaimer (exact text)

> These products are food supplements and wellness products. They are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease. Always follow the usage instructions on the pack. If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, taking medication or have a medical condition, speak to a doctor before use.

Shown on: every product page, every article (bottom), the FAQ "products" group, `/health-disclaimer`, and in short form in the footer:

> Food supplements are not medicines. Not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease. [Full disclaimer](/health-disclaimer)

## 3. Brand statements (exact text)

- Header lockup subline: **Independent KEDI Distributor**
- Footer: **[Brand Name] is an independent distributor of KEDI Healthcare products and is not the official KEDI company.**
- About page: **We are an independent distributor of KEDI Healthcare products.**
- Genuine badge: **Genuine KEDI products**, with "Sourced directly through KEDI's official distribution channel." as supporting text (confirm wording with the client).

## 4. Business opportunity (exact text)

Earnings disclaimer, shown under every business-opportunity CTA and form:

> Becoming a distributor is a business, not a job or a guaranteed income. What you earn depends entirely on your own effort, sales and KEDI's official compensation plan. We do not promise any level of income.

Never mention income figures, ranks with money attached, cars, travel, "financial freedom", "be your own boss in 30 days", or recruitment quotas.

## 5. Data protection (NDPA 2023)

### Consent text (exact)

Forms (contact, business interest, referral code):

> ☐ I agree that [Brand Name] can contact me on WhatsApp or by phone about my request. I can opt out at any time by replying STOP. [Privacy policy](/privacy)

Cart (optional; the order itself does not need consent):

> ☐ Send me usage tips and a reminder on WhatsApp when my pack is running low. Reply STOP anytime. [Privacy policy](/privacy)

Cookie bar:

> We use cookies to understand which pages help people and to measure our ads. [Privacy policy](/privacy)
> Buttons: **Accept** · **Decline**

### Rules

- Consent checkboxes start **unticked**.
- Collect only: name, phone, area, and email (contact form, optional). No date of birth, health conditions or ID numbers.
- Never put customer data into analytics events (no phone, name or order contents in GA4 or Meta).

## 6. Payment safety (exact text)

> **Pay only to our official account.** We only send payment details from our WhatsApp number [+234 800 000 0000]. Our account is **[Bank Name] · [Account Name] · [Account Number]**. If anyone asks you to pay into a different account, do not pay. Message us first.

Shown on: Contact page, FAQ (payment), cart drawer (short form), Delivery & returns page.

## 7. Formatting

| Item            | Format                                                     | Example                                     |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Money           | `₦` + thousands separators, no decimals                    | `₦18,500`                                   |
| Missing price   | —                                                          | `Ask for price`                             |
| Phone (display) | Nigerian local                                             | `0801 234 5678`                             |
| Phone (links)   | E.164 digits                                               | `wa.me/2348012345678`, `tel:+2348012345678` |
| Date            | Weekday, day month                                         | `Saturday, 12 October`                      |
| Time            | 12-hour + WAT on seminar pages                             | `4:00 PM WAT`                               |
| Pack duration   | "Lasts about N days" (or weeks if ≥ 14 and divisible by 7) | `Lasts about 4 weeks`                       |
| Order ID        | `KD-YYMMDD-XXXX`                                           | `KD-261012-7F3K`                            |

## 8. Words to prefer / avoid

| Prefer                                          | Avoid                                            |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| wellness, routine, daily habits, feel your best | cure, treatment, medicine, remedy, miracle       |
| genuine KEDI products                           | original, authentic (implies others are fake)    |
| order on WhatsApp                               | checkout, buy now                                |
| free seminar                                    | free class / free consultation (implies medical) |
| our team will guide you                         | our experts will diagnose                        |
| distributor opportunity                         | job, guaranteed income, passive income           |
