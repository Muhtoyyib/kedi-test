## About our product information

Product descriptions on this site come from the manufacturer's approved information and NAFDAC registration where applicable. We don't add our own health claims, and we don't describe products as treating, curing or preventing any disease.

## Our seminars and articles

Our free seminars and Wellness Hub articles are **general wellness education**. They are not medical advice, diagnosis or a treatment plan, and they're not a substitute for seeing a qualified health professional.

## Customer stories

Stories on this site are our customers' personal experiences of our products and service, shared with their permission. Individual experiences vary — one person's experience is not a promise of yours.

## When to see a doctor

Please speak to a doctor **before** using any product if you:

- have symptoms or a medical condition;
- are pregnant or breastfeeding;
- take any medication;
- are buying for a child.

In an emergency, go to your nearest hospital — don't wait for a reply from us.

## Reporting a reaction

If you think a product has caused a reaction:

1. Stop using the product.
2. See a doctor if you need to.
3. Tell us on WhatsApp — we'll record it and help you report it.

You can also report a suspected adverse reaction directly to **NAFDAC** through their pharmacovigilance channels.
