## What we collect

- Your name.
- Your phone / WhatsApp number.
- Your delivery area or address.
- Your email address, if you give it to us.
- What you order, and messages you send us.
- Referral codes you create or use.
- Seminar registration details (through Luma, our events provider).
- With your cookie consent only: analytics data such as pages viewed, device type and approximate location.

## Why we use it and our lawful basis

- **Fulfilling orders and delivery** — we need your details to confirm and deliver your order (contract).
- **Answering your enquiries** — you asked us something and we respond (legitimate interest / your request).
- **Follow-up tips, reorder reminders, seminar messages and referral rewards** — only where you've agreed (consent).
- **Analytics and ad measurement** — only after cookie consent (consent).
- **Preventing fraud and keeping required records** — for example tax and accounting records (legal obligation / legitimate interest).

## Who we share it with

We share only what's needed, and we never sell your data:

- **Google** — Sheets, Apps Script and Gmail store our order, lead and follow-up records.
- **Meta** — WhatsApp carries our conversations; the Facebook/Instagram pixel measures ads, with consent.
- **Luma** — seminar registrations.
- **Microsoft Clarity** — website analytics, with consent.
- **Cloudflare** — website hosting and security.
- **Paystack** — if you choose to pay by card.
- **Delivery riders and couriers** — your name, phone and address only.

## International transfers

Some of these providers store data outside Nigeria. Where that happens we rely on the safeguards those providers offer, as the NDPA allows.

## How long we keep it

- **Orders and payment records:** 6 years, for tax and accounting.
- **Customer contact records:** until 2 years after your last order, or until you ask us to delete them.
- **Enquiry messages:** 12 months.
- **Analytics:** per each provider's settings (for example, GA4 keeps data for 14 months).

## Your rights

Under the NDPA you can ask us to:

- show you the data we hold about you;
- correct anything that's wrong;
- delete your data;
- stop using your data for a purpose;
- withdraw consent at any time — reply **STOP** to any message or use **Cookie settings** in the footer;
- give you a copy of your data in a portable form.

To use any of these rights, message us on WhatsApp or email us. We'll answer within 30 days. You can also complain to the **Nigeria Data Protection Commission** (ndpc.gov.ng) if you're unhappy with how we handle your data.

## Cookies

We use cookies and similar tools only after you agree through the cookie banner: Google Analytics 4, the Meta Pixel and Microsoft Clarity. Without consent none of these run. You can change your choice any time via **Cookie settings** in the footer.

## Security

Our records live in an access-controlled Google account protected with 2-step verification. Only people who need customer data for their work can see it.

## Children

This site is not directed at children under 18, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top of this page. We'll tell you about anything significant on WhatsApp or the site.
