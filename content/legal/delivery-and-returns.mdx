## How delivery works

When you send an order on WhatsApp we confirm the delivery fee with you before you pay. We dispatch once payment is received. <DeliveryNote />

Local orders go by dispatch rider. Interstate orders go by courier (for example GIG Logistics) or park waybill — we send you the tracking details on WhatsApp.

We only ever send payment details from our official WhatsApp number. If anyone else asks you to pay, message us first — see the [payment safety notice](/contact).

## Problems with your order

If anything arrives damaged, is missing or isn't what you ordered, **message us within 48 hours of delivery** and include a photo. We'll replace the item or refund you.

## Returns

Please check your order when it arrives.

- **Unopened products** in their original condition can be returned within **[7] days** of delivery.
- You pay the return delivery cost, unless the item was wrong or damaged.
- **Opened products can't be returned** for hygiene and safety reasons, unless they're faulty.
- Refunds are paid by bank transfer within **[5] working days** of us receiving the return.
