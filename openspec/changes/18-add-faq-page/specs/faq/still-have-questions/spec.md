## Purpose

Ends the FAQ with every way to reach the business when a question isn't answered.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Contact options after the FAQ

The FAQ page SHALL end with WhatsApp, phone call and contact-page options and the usual reply time.

#### Scenario: Question not answered

- **WHEN** a visitor reaches the end of the FAQ
- **THEN** "Ask on WhatsApp", "Call {number}" and "Other ways to contact us" are offered
