## Purpose

Answers the four trust questions (genuine products, free education, follow-up, delivery) in one glance below the hero.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Four trust points

The trust bar SHALL show, in order: Genuine KEDI products, Free wellness seminars, Personal follow-up, and Delivery across {City}, each with an icon and a short supporting line.

#### Scenario: Home page

- **WHEN** a visitor scrolls past the hero
- **THEN** the four trust points are shown in that order

#### Scenario: Small screen

- **WHEN** viewed at 360px wide
- **THEN** the points are arranged two per row and every label is fully readable
