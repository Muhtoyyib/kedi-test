## Purpose

States the five reasons to buy from this distributor instead of another stockist: education, personal help, follow-up, free seminars and reliable service.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Five differentiators

The section SHALL present the five differentiators (Learn before you buy, Help choosing, We check in, Free weekly seminars, Reliable service), each with a one or two sentence explanation.

#### Scenario: Home page

- **WHEN** a visitor reaches "More than a stockist"
- **THEN** all five differentiators are listed with their explanations

### Requirement: A way to ask

When no supporting photo is supplied, the section SHALL offer a WhatsApp prompt in its place.

#### Scenario: No photo supplied

- **WHEN** the client has not supplied a supporting photo
- **THEN** a "Have a question? Ask us on WhatsApp" card is shown with a WhatsApp button
