## Content (add to `content/faq.ts`, group `seminars`, `showOn: ["seminars","faq"]`)

| Question                    | Answer                                                                                                                 |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Is the seminar really free? | Yes. Our weekly seminars are free to attend, in person or online.                                                      |
| How long is it?             | About 60 to 90 minutes, including time for questions.                                                                  |
| Can I bring someone?        | Yes, please do. Ask them to register too, so we can save them a seat and send them reminders.                          |
| Do I have to buy anything?  | No. We teach first. If you'd like to try a product afterwards, we'll help you choose, but there's no pressure.         |
| What if I can't make it?    | Message us on WhatsApp and we'll send you a summary and the next date. You can also subscribe to our seminar calendar. |
| Is it medical advice?       | No. Our seminars share general wellness information. For medical concerns, please see a doctor.                        |
| How do I join online?       | After you register, we send the YouTube or Facebook Live link by WhatsApp and email before the start.                  |
