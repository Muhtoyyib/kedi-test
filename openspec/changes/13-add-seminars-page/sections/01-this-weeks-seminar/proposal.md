## Why

Visitors arrive from a seminar ad or post and must immediately see the topic, when, where and whether it's for them, with a Register button.

## What Changes

- Hero with eyebrow ("This week's free seminar" / "Next free seminar"), topic as h1, date, time, format and venue, who should attend, a free badge, and **Register free** (scrolls to `#register` and loads the embed).
- No-upcoming state with "Get notified of every seminar" and WhatsApp.
