## Purpose

Answers the common doubts that stop people registering (cost, length, bringing someone, pressure to buy, medical advice, joining online).

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Seminar questions answered

The page SHALL answer at least: whether it is free, how long it lasts, whether guests are welcome, whether buying is required, what to do if unable to attend, whether it is medical advice, and how to join online.

#### Scenario: Pressure to buy

- **WHEN** a visitor opens "Do I have to buy anything?"
- **THEN** the answer says no and that there is no pressure

### Requirement: Ask another question

The section SHALL offer a WhatsApp link for other seminar questions.

#### Scenario: Other question

- **WHEN** a visitor taps "Ask on WhatsApp" in the seminar questions
- **THEN** WhatsApp opens with the seminar question message
