## Purpose

Ensures every product page carries the full health disclaimer required for food supplements.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Disclaimer on every product page

Every product page SHALL display the full health disclaimer text and a link to the health disclaimer page, and it SHALL NOT be hidden behind the mobile buy bar.

#### Scenario: Any product

- **WHEN** a visitor scrolls to the end of any product page on a phone
- **THEN** the full health disclaimer is fully visible above the buy bar
