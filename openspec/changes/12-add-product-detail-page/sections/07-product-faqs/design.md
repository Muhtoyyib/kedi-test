## Layout

- `id="faqs"`, h2 "Questions about {name}".
- `Accordion` group `product-faq`: product FAQs (sort order), then general items `how-to-order`, `how-to-pay`, `delivery-times` (from `getFaqs`).
- Under it: "Still have a question?" + `Button whatsapp sm` "Ask on WhatsApp" (product enquiry).

## SEO

FAQPage JSON-LD includes **only product-specific FAQs** (the general ones repeat across pages and would be duplicates).
