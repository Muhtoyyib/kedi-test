## Purpose

Defines the business opportunity page: its sections and metadata, and the rule that it makes no income claims.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: No income claims anywhere

The business opportunity page SHALL NOT state or imply income amounts, guaranteed earnings, financial freedom or lifestyle rewards, and SHALL show the earnings disclaimer near every call to action.

#### Scenario: Page audit

- **WHEN** the page text is reviewed
- **THEN** it contains no income figures or earnings promises
- **AND** the earnings disclaimer appears below the hero action and below the interest form

### Requirement: Business page metadata

The page title SHALL be "Become a KEDI distributor | {Brand}".

#### Scenario: Title

- **WHEN** the page is rendered
- **THEN** its title is "Become a KEDI distributor | {Brand}"
