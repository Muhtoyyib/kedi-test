## Purpose

Shows the steps from registering interest to starting as a distributor, with no pressure to commit.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Step-by-step path

The page SHALL list the steps: register interest, attend a business session, ask questions about registration and start-up costs, and decide in your own time.

#### Scenario: Steps shown

- **WHEN** a visitor views "How it works"
- **THEN** four numbered steps are shown, the last saying there is no pressure
