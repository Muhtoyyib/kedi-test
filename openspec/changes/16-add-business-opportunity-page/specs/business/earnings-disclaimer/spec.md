## Purpose

States plainly that being a distributor is a business with no guaranteed income.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Earnings disclaimer

The page SHALL display the exact earnings disclaimer stating that becoming a distributor is a business, that earnings depend on the person's own effort and KEDI's official plan, and that no level of income is promised.

#### Scenario: End of page

- **WHEN** a visitor reaches the end of the business page
- **THEN** the "About earnings" disclaimer is visible with the exact approved text
