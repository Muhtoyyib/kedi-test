## Purpose

Invites prospective distributors to the business session at the end of the weekly seminar.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Invitation with next date

The page SHALL invite visitors to the business session of the weekly seminar, showing the next seminar's date, time and format when one is scheduled, with a Register action and a WhatsApp alternative.

#### Scenario: Seminar scheduled

- **WHEN** a seminar is scheduled for Saturday 12 October at 4:00 PM
- **THEN** the invitation shows "Next: Saturday, 12 October · 4:00 PM WAT"

#### Scenario: None scheduled

- **WHEN** no seminar is scheduled
- **THEN** the invitation suggests asking on WhatsApp for the next date
