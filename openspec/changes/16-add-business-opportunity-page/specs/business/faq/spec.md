## Purpose

Answers prospective distributors' common questions honestly, including that no income is promised.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Honest business answers

The page SHALL answer at least: experience needed, part-time, start-up cost, earnings, recruiting, and help finding customers, and the earnings answer SHALL state that no income is promised.

#### Scenario: Earnings question

- **WHEN** a visitor opens "How much can I earn?"
- **THEN** the answer begins "We don't promise any income."
