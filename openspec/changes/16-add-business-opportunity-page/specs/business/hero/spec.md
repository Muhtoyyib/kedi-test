## Purpose

Introduces the distributor opportunity honestly and offers registering interest or attending the business session.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Honest introduction with actions

The hero SHALL describe the opportunity as becoming an independent KEDI distributor with training and support, offer "Register your interest" and "Attend the business session", and show the earnings disclaimer beneath them.

#### Scenario: Register interest

- **WHEN** a visitor taps "Register your interest"
- **THEN** the page scrolls to the interest form
