## Purpose

Explains in three steps how referring a friend works, from getting a link to receiving the reward.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Three-step explanation

The page SHALL explain: get your link, share it (the code applies automatically for the friend), and both get rewarded after the friend's first paid order.

#### Scenario: Steps shown

- **WHEN** a visitor views "How it works"
- **THEN** the three steps are shown in that order
