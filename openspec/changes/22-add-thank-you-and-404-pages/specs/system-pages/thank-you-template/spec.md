## Purpose

Confirms a successful submission, explains what happens next, and offers a next step and social follows.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Clear confirmation

Every thank-you page SHALL confirm success in its heading, explain what happens next, offer a relevant next step and a link home, and SHALL NOT be indexed by search engines.

#### Scenario: After a form

- **WHEN** a visitor lands on any thank-you page
- **THEN** the heading confirms success, focus is on the heading, and the page carries a noindex directive
