## Purpose

Defines the contact page: every way to reach the business, its location, a contact form and payment safety information.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Contact page content

/contact SHALL present, in order: contact methods (WhatsApp first), location with hours and map, the contact form, and the payment safety notice.

#### Scenario: Visit contact

- **WHEN** a visitor opens /contact on a phone
- **THEN** the WhatsApp action is the first action on the page
