## Purpose

Ends every article with the three next steps the plan requires: attend the free seminar, view related products, or chat on WhatsApp.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Three next steps

Every article SHALL end with options to register for the free seminar (with the next date when scheduled), view products (the related category when set, otherwise the shop), and chat on WhatsApp.

#### Scenario: Seminar scheduled

- **WHEN** the next seminar is on Saturday 12 October
- **THEN** the seminar option reads "Ask questions at our free seminar on Saturday, 12 October"

#### Scenario: No related category

- **WHEN** an article has no related category
- **THEN** the products option links to the shop
