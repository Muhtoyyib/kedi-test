## Purpose

Introduces the Wellness Hub as practical, non-medical guidance and features the newest article.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Purpose and featured article

The hub SHALL state that its content is general information, not medical advice, and SHALL feature the newest article when no topic filter is applied.

#### Scenario: Default hub view

- **WHEN** a visitor opens /learn
- **THEN** the heading, the "not medical advice" note and the newest article as a featured card are shown
