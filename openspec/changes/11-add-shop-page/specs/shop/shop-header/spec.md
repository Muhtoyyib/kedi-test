## Purpose

Introduces the shop and explains in three steps how ordering through WhatsApp works.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Ordering explained up front

The shop page SHALL explain, before the product list, that customers add products to a cart, send the order on WhatsApp, and pay to the business account before delivery.

#### Scenario: First visit to the shop

- **WHEN** a visitor opens /shop
- **THEN** the heading "Genuine KEDI products" and the three ordering steps are shown above the products
- **AND** a link to delivery areas and fees is available
