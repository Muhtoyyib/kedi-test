## Purpose

Offers shoppers who can't decide a direct route to advice on WhatsApp or at the free seminar.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Help after the product list

Every shop listing SHALL end with "Not sure which to choose?", offering a WhatsApp chat and a link to the seminar dates.

#### Scenario: End of listing

- **WHEN** a visitor scrolls past the last product
- **THEN** they see "Not sure which to choose?" with "Ask on WhatsApp" and "See seminar dates"
