## Purpose

Invites existing customers to share their experience on WhatsApp, with a clear promise about consent.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Story invitation with consent promise

The stories page SHALL end with an invitation to share an experience on WhatsApp, stating that nothing is published without the customer's agreement.

#### Scenario: Customer wants to share

- **WHEN** a visitor taps "Share on WhatsApp"
- **THEN** WhatsApp opens with the story submission message
