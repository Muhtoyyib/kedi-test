## Purpose

Shows consented video testimonials without slowing the page.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Video testimonials

The page SHALL show each video story as a preview with the customer's first name, area and quote, loading the video only when tapped, and SHALL omit the section when there are none.

#### Scenario: Two video stories

- **WHEN** two video stories are published
- **THEN** two previews are shown and no video loads until tapped
