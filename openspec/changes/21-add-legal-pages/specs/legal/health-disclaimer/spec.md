## Purpose

Provides the full health disclaimer and guidance on when to see a doctor, linked from product pages, articles and the footer.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Full disclaimer page

/health-disclaimer SHALL show the exact health disclaimer and explain that seminars and articles are general information, that stories are personal experiences, when to see a doctor, and how to report a reaction.

#### Scenario: Footer link

- **WHEN** a visitor taps "Full disclaimer" in the footer
- **THEN** /health-disclaimer opens with the exact disclaimer at the top
