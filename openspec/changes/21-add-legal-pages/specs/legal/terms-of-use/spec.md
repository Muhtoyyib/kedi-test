## Purpose

Sets out the terms for using the site and ordering through WhatsApp, including that the business is independent of KEDI.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Ordering terms

The terms SHALL explain that a WhatsApp order is a request, that the agreement forms when stock, price and delivery fee are confirmed and payment is made, and that the confirmed price applies.

#### Scenario: Price changed after adding to cart

- **WHEN** a customer reads the terms about prices
- **THEN** they learn that the price confirmed on WhatsApp is the one that applies

### Requirement: Independence and governing law

The terms SHALL state that the business is an independent distributor and not KEDI, and that Nigerian law governs.

#### Scenario: Terms review

- **WHEN** the terms are read
- **THEN** the independent distributor statement and Nigerian governing law are present
