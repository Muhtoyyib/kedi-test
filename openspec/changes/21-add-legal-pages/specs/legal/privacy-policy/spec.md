## Purpose

Tells visitors what personal data the business collects, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and their rights under the NDPA 2023.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: NDPA notice contents

The privacy policy SHALL identify the business as data controller with contact details, list the data collected, the purposes and lawful bases, the third parties data is shared with, international transfers, retention periods, data subject rights including complaining to the NDPC, cookie use and how to change consent.

#### Scenario: Rights section

- **WHEN** a visitor reads the privacy policy
- **THEN** it explains how to access, correct or delete their data and how to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission

### Requirement: Linked from every consent

Every consent checkbox and the cookie bar SHALL link to the privacy policy.

#### Scenario: Contact form consent

- **WHEN** a visitor views the contact form consent checkbox
- **THEN** a "Privacy policy" link to /privacy is next to it
