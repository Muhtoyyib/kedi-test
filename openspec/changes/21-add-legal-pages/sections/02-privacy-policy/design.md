## Required headings and content (draft for client/lawyer review)

1. **Who we are:** [Registered Business Name], an independent KEDI distributor at [address]; data controller; contact hello@[domain] / WhatsApp.
2. **What we collect:** name, phone/WhatsApp number, delivery area/address, email (optional), order contents, referral codes, messages you send us, seminar registration details (via Luma), and, only with cookie consent, analytics data (pages viewed, device, approximate location).
3. **Why we use it and our lawful basis:** fulfilling orders and delivery (contract); answering enquiries (legitimate interest / your request); follow-up tips and reorder reminders, seminar messages and referral rewards (consent); analytics and ad measurement (consent); preventing fraud and keeping records (legal obligation / legitimate interest).
4. **Who we share it with:** Google (Sheets, Apps Script, Gmail: storage and email), Meta (WhatsApp; Facebook/Instagram pixel with consent), Luma (seminar registration), Microsoft Clarity (with consent), Cloudflare (website hosting), Paystack (if you pay by card), delivery riders and couriers (name, phone, address only). We don't sell your data.
5. **International transfers:** some providers store data outside Nigeria; we rely on their safeguards as permitted by the NDPA.
6. **How long we keep it:** orders and payment records for 6 years (tax/accounting); customer contact records until 2 years after your last order or until you ask us to delete them; enquiry messages 12 months; analytics per the providers' settings (GA4 retention 14 months).
7. **Your rights:** access, correction, deletion, objection, withdrawing consent (reply STOP or tap Cookie settings), data portability, and complaining to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng). How to exercise them: WhatsApp or email; reply within 30 days.
8. **Cookies:** what we use (GA4, Meta Pixel, Clarity) only after consent; how to change your choice (footer "Cookie settings").
9. **Security:** access-controlled Google account with 2-step verification; only staff who need it can see customer data.
10. **Children:** not directed at under-18s; we don't knowingly collect their data.
11. **Changes to this policy:** we'll update the date above.
