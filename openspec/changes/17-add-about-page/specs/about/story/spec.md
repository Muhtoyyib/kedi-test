## Purpose

Introduces the distributor personally with a photo, role, experience and story.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Personal introduction

The About page SHALL open with the distributor's photo, a greeting using their first name, their role as an independent KEDI distributor in the city, their years of experience and their story.

#### Scenario: About page top

- **WHEN** a visitor opens /about
- **THEN** they see the distributor's photo and "Hi, I'm {first name}" with the role and years of experience
