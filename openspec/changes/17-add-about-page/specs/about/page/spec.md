## Purpose

Defines the About page: its sections and metadata, telling who runs the business and how it relates to KEDI.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: About page content

/about SHALL present the distributor's story, mission and values, the independent distributor statement, location and service areas, and a closing call to action.

#### Scenario: Visit about

- **WHEN** a visitor opens /about
- **THEN** all five sections appear in that order
