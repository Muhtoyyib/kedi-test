## Purpose

Invites About-page readers to meet the distributor at a seminar or message them.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Meet or message

The About page SHALL end with "Come and say hello", offering seminar dates and a WhatsApp chat.

#### Scenario: End of About page

- **WHEN** a visitor reaches the end of /about
- **THEN** "See seminar dates" and "Chat on WhatsApp" are offered
