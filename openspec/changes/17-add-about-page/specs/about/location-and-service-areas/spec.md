## Purpose

Shows where the business is, when it is open, and which areas it delivers to.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Location, hours and areas

The About page SHALL show the business address with a directions link, the opening hours, the local service areas, and a link to delivery fees and times.

#### Scenario: Get directions

- **WHEN** a visitor taps "Get directions"
- **THEN** Google Maps opens with the business location search
