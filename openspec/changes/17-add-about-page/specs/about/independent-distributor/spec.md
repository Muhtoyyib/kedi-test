## Purpose

Makes clear that the business is an independent distributor of KEDI products, not KEDI itself, and who handles customers' orders.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Exact independence statement

The About page SHALL display the sentence "We are an independent distributor of KEDI Healthcare products." and explain that orders, payments, delivery and follow-up are handled by the business.

#### Scenario: About page

- **WHEN** a visitor reads the About page
- **THEN** the exact independence sentence is shown with the explanation of who handles their order
